Challenges solar powered refrigeration:
- Solar power is limited by the time in direct sunlight, while cooling is required 24/7
- Refrigeration is limited by the ability to reject extra heat from the sun
- Refrigerant leaks can destroy computer hardware
- Requires a lot of open land/real estate
- The data center will require uneven cooling across server racks
- The initial cost of the infrastructure is high
- Solar panels are less effective at high temperatures
- High maintenance requirement 

Problem Statement for Solar-powered refrigeration for Data Centers

The goal is to develop a solar-powered refrigeration system for cooling data centers that can maintain the CPUs and GPUs between 20C and 28C for 24 hours per day at all times, with less than 10 square miles of usable solar area and by generating more than 10MW of power and X tonnes of cooling to the data center.
